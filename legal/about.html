About Us

Atlanta Bounce House Rentals is an independent online directory that helps families, schools, churches, businesses and event planners across Atlanta, Georgia find and compare local bounce house and party rental providers in one convenient place.

Our Mission

Planning a party should be fun, not frustrating. Our mission is to make it simple to discover reputable rental companies in the Atlanta metro, compare the services they offer and request free quotes, all without spending hours searching across dozens of websites and phone calls.

What We Do

We maintain an organized directory of bounce house and party rental services available throughout Atlanta and surrounding communities, including classic bounce houses, bounce-and-slide combos, water slides, obstacle courses, concessions, tents, tables and chairs, interactive games, full party packages and event staffing.

When you submit a quote request, we connect you with providers in our network who serve your area and offer the rentals you need. We do not own the inflatables ourselves; instead, we focus on connecting customers with the right local companies.

Why Use a Directory?

  • Save time: Compare multiple Atlanta providers from a single request.
  • Transparent pricing: See typical small, medium and large event price ranges before you book.
  • Local focus: Every provider serves the Atlanta, Georgia metro area.
  • No obligation: Quotes are always free and you choose who to book.

Serving the Atlanta Metro

Our directory covers Atlanta and surrounding communities including Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, Alpharetta, Smyrna, East Point, College Park, Dunwoody and more.
