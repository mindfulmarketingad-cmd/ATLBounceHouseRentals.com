Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Larry The Barber Grooming Co. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Booking information you provide: name, phone number, email address, requested service, preferred date and time, and any notes you include when requesting an appointment.
  • Contact information: any details you share when you call, text, or email us.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited, collected through standard web server logs and any analytics tools described in our Cookie Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule, or cancel your appointments.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your appointment.
  • To respond to questions and provide customer service.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How Booking Requests Are Processed

Appointment requests submitted through our booking form are delivered to us by a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). Your submission is transmitted to our email so we can confirm your appointment. Form processors handle your data only to deliver your message to us.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate this website (such as hosting and form delivery), when required by law, or to protect our rights. We never share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Text and Phone Communications

By providing your phone number in a booking request, you consent to receive calls or text messages from us regarding your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

6. Data Retention

We keep booking and contact information only as long as needed to provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. Appointments for minors should be booked by a parent or guardian.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
