Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Larry The Barber Grooming Co. uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve user experience, and provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

This website is designed to be lightweight and currently does not set its own cookies. Cookies or similar technologies may be used by:

  • Hosting and security providers — strictly necessary technologies that keep the site online and secure.
  • Analytics tools — if enabled in the future, to help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g., pages visited, time on site).
  • Third-party services — links to external sites such as our product storefront may set their own cookies once you visit them, governed by their own policies.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of a website function. For instructions, check the help section of your browser.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we add analytics or other cookie-setting tools, we will update this page with details of the cookies used and their purposes.
