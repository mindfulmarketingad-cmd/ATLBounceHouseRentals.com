Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Larry The Barber Grooming Co. is committed to making this website accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability.

Our Commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Measures we take include:

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks.
  • Descriptive alternative text on images.
  • Labeled form fields in our online booking form.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Keyboard-navigable links, buttons, and form controls.
  • Responsive layouts that work on mobile devices and at increased zoom levels.

Booking by Phone

If any part of the online booking experience is difficult to use, you can always book directly by calling or texting (404) 490-3052.

Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter a barrier, please contact us at (404) 490-3052 and we will work to address it promptly.
