About Party Package Rentals in Atlanta

A party package is a pre-built bundle — for example a bounce house plus six tables, forty-eight chairs and a popcorn machine — designed to cover a typical Atlanta backyard party from start to finish at a discounted rate.

Packages take the guesswork out of planning. Tell a directory provider your guest count and theme, and they will recommend the right combination of inflatables, seating and concessions, then deliver and set up everything at once.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver party package rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
