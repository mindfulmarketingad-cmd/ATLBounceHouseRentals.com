About Interactive Rentals in Atlanta

Interactive inflatables and games are built around head-to-head play: gladiator joust, bungee basketball, axe-throwing (foam), Velcro sticky walls, dunk tanks and giant connect-four. They are ideal when guests want to do more than just bounce.

These rentals are popular for Atlanta school carnivals, church youth nights, grad parties and team-building events. Providers deliver, set up and explain safe play for each game.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver interactive rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
