About Bounce and Slide Combo Rentals in Atlanta

Combo units are the most requested rental across the Atlanta metro for a reason: a single inflatable delivers a bounce floor, a climbing wall and a slide, often with a basketball hoop and obstacle pop-ups inside. That variety keeps children moving and reduces lines compared with a standard bounce house.

Atlanta combo rentals come in dry and wet/dry configurations. In the warm Georgia months, many families choose a wet/dry combo so the slide can be run with water for extra summer fun. Providers in our directory deliver, set up, anchor and sanitize every unit before your event.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver bounce and slide combo rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
