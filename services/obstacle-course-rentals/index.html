About Obstacle Course Rentals in Atlanta

Inflatable obstacle courses challenge guests to race head-to-head through a series of squeeze walls, pop-up barriers, climb-and-slide exits and crawl tunnels. Single-lane and dual-lane racing options are available to suit your crowd size.

Because of their length, obstacle courses are popular for larger Atlanta venues, school grounds and open parks. Directory providers handle delivery, layout, anchoring and supervision guidance so your event runs safely.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver obstacle course rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
