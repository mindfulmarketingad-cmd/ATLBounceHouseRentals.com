About Photo Booth Rentals in Atlanta

Photo booth styles available from Atlanta rental providers include classic enclosed booths with a curtain and strip prints, open-air setups that accommodate larger groups, 360-degree spinning photo platforms that capture slow-motion video clips, and roaming camera operators who circulate through your event with a handheld rig. Most setups include a prop table with themed accessories, a digital touchscreen interface, instant on-site printing and a password-protected online gallery shared with all guests after the event. Custom branded overlays with your event name, logo or date are available for corporate events and weddings.

Photo booths are popular at Atlanta weddings, corporate holiday parties, quinceañeras, sweet 16 parties and school proms. They provide built-in entertainment during cocktail hour or reception lulls, and guests consistently rank them among the most enjoyed elements of any event. Rental packages typically run 2 to 4 hours, with overtime available. Book 3 to 6 weeks ahead for popular weekend dates across the Atlanta metro.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver photo booth rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
