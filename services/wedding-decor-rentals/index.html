About Wedding and Event Decor Rentals in Atlanta

Atlanta wedding and event decor rentals span a wide range of furnishings and styling elements. Chair options include gold and white chiavari chairs, clear ghost chairs, cross-back wood chairs and throne sweetheart chairs. Table selections include standard banquet tables, round tables, farmhouse harvest tables and cocktail high-tops. Linen rentals cover fitted spandex covers, full-length satin or polyester tablecloths, sashes, napkins and specialty overlays. Drapery and backdrop rentals add pipe-and-drape walls, sheer curtain panels, sequin backdrops and balloon arch frames that transform plain venues into styled spaces. Many providers also offer decorative lighting including Edison string lights, fairy light curtains and LED uplighting for tent events.

Coordinating decor rentals with your other event rentals simplifies logistics and can reduce costs. Many Atlanta providers that offer furniture rentals also carry inflatables, tents and concession equipment, making it possible to book everything through a single company with one delivery. When planning a wedding or large event, request a full decor inventory list from providers and compare packages at least 8 to 12 weeks in advance to secure popular items like chiavari chairs and farm tables, which book out quickly during Atlanta's spring and fall wedding seasons.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver wedding and event decor rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
