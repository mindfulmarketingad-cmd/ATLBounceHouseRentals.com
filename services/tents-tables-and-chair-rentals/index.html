About Tents, Tables and Chair Rentals in Atlanta

Event tents range from 10x10 pop-up canopies to large 20x40 frame tents, paired with sturdy folding tables and chairs. They protect guests from the Georgia sun and summer showers while defining your event space.

Directory providers across Atlanta deliver, install and break down tents, and offer table linens, sidewalls and lighting as add-ons. Reserve early in spring and graduation season when demand is highest.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver tents, tables and chair rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
