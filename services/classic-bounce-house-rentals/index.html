About Classic Bounce House Rentals in Atlanta

A classic bounce house is a fully enclosed inflatable jumping area with safety netting on all sides. It is the most budget-friendly rental in Atlanta and fits comfortably in most yards, driveways, parks and indoor gyms.

Themed castle, sports and character designs are available from directory providers throughout Atlanta and Georgia. Each unit is professionally cleaned, delivered, staked or sandbagged and tested before guests arrive.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver classic bounce house rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
