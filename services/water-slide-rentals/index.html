About Water Slide Rentals in Atlanta

Inflatable water slides connect to a standard garden hose and feature a splash pool or slip lane at the bottom. They range from kid-friendly low slides to giant climb-and-plunge slides for teens and adults.

Given Atlanta's long warm season, water slides are one of the most booked summer rentals in Georgia. Reserve early for weekends. Providers deliver, set up on a suitable grassy area and confirm water hookup before the party begins.

Serving all of metro Atlanta. Providers in our directory deliver water slide rentals to Atlanta, Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, East Point and surrounding Georgia communities.
